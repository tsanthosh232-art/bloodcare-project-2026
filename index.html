Hemoglobin Levels

Understand hemoglobin and its role in the human body.

What is Hemoglobin?

Hemoglobin is an iron-containing protein in red blood cells. It carries oxygen from the lungs to the body's tissues. It is commonly measured in grams per deciliter (g/dL).

Adult men13.5–17.5 g/dL
Adult women (not pregnant)12.0–15.5 g/dL
PregnancyVaries by trimester
Why is it important?
Hemoglobin helps deliver oxygen throughout the body. Low or high results should be interpreted by a qualified healthcare professional using the lab's reference range and the person's circumstances.
🫁
Oxygen transport

Hemoglobin carries oxygen from the lungs to body tissues.

🩸
Red blood cells

Hemoglobin gives red blood cells much of their red colour.

🧪
Blood test

A complete blood count (CBC) commonly includes a hemoglobin measurement.

Reference values are approximate educational examples. Ranges differ between laboratories and may vary with age, pregnancy and other factors. This website cannot diagnose anemia.

Blood Components

Blood has four major components, each with an important function.

🔴

Red Blood Cells

Carry oxygen from the lungs to the rest of the body with the help of hemoglobin.

OXYGEN TRANSPORT
⚪

White Blood Cells

Help the immune system defend the body against infections and harmful germs.

BODY DEFENCE
🟣

Platelets

Help blood clot and support the body in stopping bleeding after an injury.

CLOTTING
🟡

Plasma

The liquid part of blood. It transports nutrients, hormones, proteins and waste products.

TRANSPORT

Why are blood components important?

Each component performs a different job. Together, they help the body transport oxygen, fight infection, control bleeding and maintain normal body functions. In medical care, some patients may receive specific blood components rather than whole blood.

GIVE THE GIFT OF LIFE

Donate Blood. Help Save Lives.

Blood donation supports patients who need blood during surgery, accidents, childbirth complications and certain medical treatments. Eligible donors can make a meaningful difference.

❤️🩸

Before You Donate

General awareness tips for potential blood donors.

🥗
Eat well

Have a healthy meal and follow the donation centre's instructions.

💧
Stay hydrated

Drink enough water before and after donation, as advised.

🩺
Health screening

Donation staff will check your eligibility and health before donation.

🪪
Bring identification

Carry the identification documents requested by the donation centre.

🛌
Take rest

Follow post-donation advice and avoid strenuous activity for the recommended period.

🏥
Choose a licensed centre

Donate only at an authorized blood centre with trained staff.

Donation eligibility, age, weight, hemoglobin requirements and donation intervals depend on local regulations and individual health. Always follow the screening team's advice. Never rely on this educational website to decide eligibility.

About BloodCare

A student mini project focused on health awareness.

OUR PROJECT

Knowledge Can Save Lives.

BloodCare is an educational website designed to make basic information about blood groups, hemoglobin, blood components and donation easier to understand.

🩺

🎯 Project Objective

To spread awareness about blood groups, hemoglobin levels, the functions of blood components and the importance of safe blood donation.

📚 What We Cover

ABO and Rh blood types, general hemoglobin reference ranges, the four major blood components and basic blood donation awareness.

💡 Project Purpose

To present health-related educational information in a simple, organized and accessible website for students and the public.

⚕️ Medical Disclaimer

This is an educational student project, not a medical service. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, donor matching or live blood-bank availability.
